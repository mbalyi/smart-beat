# Nu-metal and modern-metal sounds: what we can bundle, import or point to

Research for [#34](https://github.com/mbalyi/smart-beat/issues/34), [#41](https://github.com/mbalyi/smart-beat/issues/41) and [#42](https://github.com/mbalyi/smart-beat/issues/42) (October 2026). It covers free and open-source compressors, pedals, amps, cabinets and EQ presets for nu-metal, metalcore, hardcore, deathcore and djent, and what our licence policy ([ADR 0001](adr/0001-project-license.md)) allows us to do with each.

**Short version:**

- **Bundle** only our own presets (parameter values) and our own DSP. That includes algorithms ported from MIT-licensed Airwindows code, with attribution.
- **Import** covers amp, pedal and cabinet recordings (`.nam` captures, impulse responses). The user brings them; they are never bundled.
- **Point to** closed freeware plugins. They need plugin hosting first, which is a later, opt-in part of #41.

## Recommendations

1. **Next to bundle:**
   - a reverb and a delay (our own DSP, for the ambient clean layer and leads);
   - an Airwindows-based bus compressor and saturation for the master chain.
2. **Ask Jester Dyne Productions** for written permission to bundle the Brutal and Emerald packs. They would replace two generated cabinets with recorded ones.
3. **Keep NAM and IR files user-supplied.** Possibly add a "find captures" link to TONE3000 in the sound library dialog, opened in the user's browser. The user downloads files themselves and nothing is fetched automatically.
4. **Plugin hosting** for the freeware in section 3 stays opt-in and out of process (#41).
